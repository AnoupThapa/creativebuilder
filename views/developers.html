Keys & authentication

Secret keys (pf_live_…) are for your servers only — never put them in a web page or app. Send them in the Authorization header. They act as the team member who created them: every image you get back counts as one download from that person's allowance, exactly like in the app.

Publishable keys (pf_pub_…) only power the website widget, and only on the websites you list.

curl /api/v1/me -H "Authorization: Bearer pf_live_YOUR_KEY"

POST /api/v1/resize

Fit one photo into one or more platform sizes. One size returns the image; several return a ZIP.

FieldValues
imageThe photo (multipart file, JPG/PNG/WEBP, up to 15 MB)
sizesComma list, e.g. ig_post,ig_story,fb_post — see GET /api/v1/sizes
fitcontain (whole product visible, default) or cover (fill & crop)
backgroundblur (default), white, color + color=#hex, brand + brand_id, transparent (PNG only)
formatjpg (default) or png
curl /api/v1/resize -H "Authorization: Bearer pf_live_YOUR_KEY" \
  -F image=@candle.jpg -F sizes=ig_post,ig_story,fb_post -F background=blur -o sizes.zip

POST /api/v1/remove-background

Removes the background and returns a transparent PNG (up to 1200 px). Works best on plain or studio backgrounds. Counts as one download.

curl /api/v1/remove-background -H "Authorization: Bearer pf_live_YOUR_KEY" -F image=@product.jpg -o cutout.png

POST /api/v1/captions

Captions with hashtags written for each platform, in your language. JSON body: product, details, price, offer, brand, contact, language (e.g. Nepali), tone (friendly | professional | fun | luxury | urgent), platforms (any of instagram, facebook, tiktok, linkedin, x, whatsapp, pinterest, youtube). Up to 100 a day per person.

curl /api/v1/captions -H "Authorization: Bearer pf_live_YOUR_KEY" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"product":"Rose face serum","price":"Rs. 1,250","language":"Nepali","platforms":["instagram","facebook"]}'

AI product photos

GET /api/v1/ai/styles lists the styles. POST /api/v1/ai/images (multipart: style, image, product, details, aspect = 4:5 | 1:1 | 9:16 | 16:9, count 1–2) starts a job and returns its id — images never contain any text. Poll GET /api/v1/ai/jobs/{id} every few seconds until status is done, then download each URL in images with your key. Uses your AI credits; failed images are refunded.

Other endpoints

GET /api/v1/me — workspace, plan and downloads left · GET /api/v1/sizes — all platform sizes · GET /api/v1/brands — your brand kits (colours, fonts, contact details).

Errors & limits

Errors are JSON: {"error": "plain-English message", "code": "…"}. Common codes: auth (401), quota / upgrade (402), rate_limit (429, 120 requests a minute per key), bad_request (400). The header X-PostGenX-Downloads-Left tells you what's left.

Website widget

Add a free “resize your photo for every social platform” tool to your website, in your name and colour (set in White-label review portal). Create a publishable key with your website address, then paste:

<script src="/widget.js" data-key="pf_pub_YOUR_KEY" async></script>

The photo is processed in the visitor's browser. Each image they download counts as one download from your allowance; when it runs out the tool asks visitors to come back tomorrow. It only appears on the websites listed on the key.
