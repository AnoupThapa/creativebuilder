Questions

Straight answers.

Can’t find yours? Ask us — a real person replies.

What counts as one image?

Each downloaded file counts once. Generating 5 platform sizes in one ZIP uses 5 images. Editing and previewing are always free and unlimited.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. PostGenX runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers — nothing to install. Downloads save straight to your phone so you can post from the Instagram or Facebook app.

Which plan should I pick?

Starter (US$9: 5 images a day, 40 AI credits a month, your own videos up to 30 s) suits most single-location shops. Pro (US$24) adds 15 a day, AI product videos, AI model presenter videos, longer videos and print quality. Business and Agency are for multi-store brands, wholesalers and agencies — up to 50 brands, client logins and bulk posting.

Will the AI put spelling mistakes on my pictures?

No. AI pictures and videos are made with no writing at all — your headline, price and logo are added afterwards by PostGenX, sharp and correctly spelled, in any language. Every AI image is checked automatically; anything with stray writing or a changed product is redone or refunded.

Can agencies give clients their own login?

Yes. On a Business account, invite a client for their brand — the login is free (no seat), and they only see and download their own brand's designs.

What does "per user" mean?

Your subscription is priced per seat. Each team member you add gets their own allowance on your plan.

Can I post videos?

Yes. From Starter you can turn your own videos (up to 30 seconds) into branded MP4 posts; Pro and above allow longer videos and AI product videos made from a photo.

Which languages can I use?

Type your posts in any language — PostGenX picks fonts that display Nepali, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Sinhala, Thai, Arabic, Urdu and more correctly. AI captions can be written in your language too.

What happens to my data?

Your photos and designs are private to your team. You can download everything or delete your account yourself at any time from Account & security. We use cookie-free analytics and never sell data.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Any time from Billing. Cancelling keeps your plan active until the end of the period you've paid for.
