Questions

What counts as one image?

Each downloaded file counts once. Generating 5 platform sizes in one ZIP uses 5 images. Editing and previewing are always free and unlimited.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. PostForge runs in the browser on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers — nothing to install. Downloads save straight to your phone so you can post from the Instagram or Facebook app.

Which plan should I pick?

Starter gives each user up to 5 images a day (100 a month) — enough for most single-location businesses posting daily. Pro gives 15 a day (300 a month), video posts and every template, for busier businesses or agencies.

What does "per user" mean?

Your subscription is priced per seat. Each team member you add gets their own allowance on your plan.

Can I post videos?

Yes — the Pro plan lets you add your branding and offer to short customer videos and download them as MP4, the format Instagram, Facebook and TikTok expect.

What happens to my data?

Your photos and designs are private to your team. You can download everything or delete your account yourself at any time from Account & security. We use cookie-free analytics and never sell data.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Any time from Billing. Cancelling keeps your plan active until the end of the period you've paid for.
