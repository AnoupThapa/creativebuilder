🚀 Getting started

How do I make my first post?
  1. Sign up and confirm your email (check spam if it doesn't arrive).
  2. Click New design, upload a photo, then pick a template or type your offer.
  3. Choose a size (e.g. Instagram Post) at the top of the canvas and click Download.
Which sizes are supported?

Instagram post, portrait & story/reels, Facebook post, story & cover, LinkedIn post & banner, Pinterest, X (Twitter), TikTok, YouTube thumbnail & Shorts, and WhatsApp status. Use Generate all platforms to get every size in one ZIP.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — PostGenX works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. For the most comfortable editing use a tablet or computer, but everything, including downloads, works on a phone.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or promotions folder. In the dashboard, click Resend email on the yellow banner. Still nothing after 10 minutes? Contact us below.

🎨 Designing posts

How do I move text, the badge or the logo?

Click it on the design and drag it. Drag the round corner handle to resize. For precise moves use the arrow pad and sliders in the Layout tab, or pick a one-click Layout (Classic, Centred, Text on top, Middle) under Style.

How do I remove the background from a product photo?

Switch on Remove background under your photo. If anything needs fixing, click ✨ Refine: drag a Subject box around the product for busy backgrounds, then use the Keep and Erase brushes or Magic erase. Choose a backdrop (gradient, spotlight, blurred photo) behind the cut-out.

Can I use my own logo, colours and fonts?

Yes. Save a Brand kit (logo, colour, phone, website) in the dashboard or the editor's Brand Kit tab and apply it in one click. Pick heading and text fonts under Style.

Can I make video posts?

On the Pro plan, upload a video (MP4, MOV or WEBM), add your branding, choose MP4 video in Export and download. Videos come out as MP4 (H.264 + AAC) — the format Instagram, Facebook and TikTok expect.

How do I make a customer-review post?

Choose Customer review at the top of the left panel, type the review, the customer's name and star rating, and add their photo if you have permission to use it.

⬇ Downloads & allowances

What counts as one image?

Every file you download counts once. A ZIP with 4 sizes uses 4. Editing and previewing are always unlimited.

When does my allowance reset?

Daily limits reset at midnight in the time zone set in Account & security. Monthly limits reset on the 1st.

Why is there a watermark?

Free-trial downloads carry a small watermark. Any paid plan removes it.

My download looks blurry

Use a photo at least 1080 px on its shortest side, and choose HD 2× (Starter/Pro) or Print 3× (Pro) in Export.

📣 Posting to Facebook & Instagram

How do I connect my Facebook Page and Instagram?

Go to Social posts and click Connect Facebook & Instagram (owners and admins only). Facebook opens its own login window — you sign in there, choose the Pages and Instagram accounts to share, and approve. PostGenX never sees your password.

Why doesn't my Instagram account show up?

Instagram posting works for Business or Creator accounts that are linked to a Facebook Page. In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → switch to Professional, then link it to your Facebook Page. Connect again in PostGenX and tick the Page.

How do I post or schedule?

Open a design, click 📣 Post, tick the accounts, write a caption, then choose Post now or Schedule and pick a date and time. Scheduled posts are listed under Social posts, where you can change the time or cancel them.

Which sizes work on Instagram?

Feed posts must be between portrait 4:5 and landscape 1.91:1 — use Instagram Post (square) or Instagram Portrait. Tall 9:16 designs (Story/Reels size) can be posted as an Instagram Story, or as a Reel when the design is a video.

Does posting use my download allowance?

Yes — each post counts as one download, however many accounts it goes to.

A post failed — what now?

Open Social posts → Posted & history; the reason is shown under the post. If it says the connection expired, click Connect Facebook & Instagram again, then Retry.

💳 Plans & billing

What do the plans include?

Free — try every tool: 5 watermarked downloads and 5 AI photos. Starter — US$9 per user per month: 5 images a day (up to 100 a month), HD quality, all templates, your own videos up to 30 seconds, 40 AI credits a month. Pro — US$24 per user per month: 15 images a day (up to 300 a month), video posts, print quality, 5 brand kits, 150 AI credits a month, AI videos and AI model presenter videos. Business — US$59 per user per month: 50 a day (up to 1,000 a month), 20 brand kits, 400 AI credits a month, developer API and website widget. Agency — US$119 per user per month: 50 client brands, white-label client review portal, 800 AI credits a month. Yearly billing gives you 2 months free. One AI photo uses 1 credit, an AI product video 10, an 8-second presenter video 15.

How do AI product photos work?

Open ✨ AI Studio, pick your industry and a style, then add a photo of your product (or paste its web link). The AI makes a professional picture around your real product — without any writing on it. Click Add text & logo to finish it in the editor. One picture uses one AI credit. If the AI fails, the credit is returned automatically. Need more? Buy a pack of 100 credits for US$12 (never expires).

How do I send designs to a client for approval?

On a Business account with a paid plan: open a design, choose the client's brand, and click 📤 Send for review. Pick the sizes and add a note. Then in Brand kits click 🔗 Review link on that client's brand and send them the link. They see your name, logo and colour (set them in Account & security → White-label review portal) — never PostGenX. They can approve, ask for changes, comment and download, with no login. You get an email for each reply, and you can turn a link off at any time. Each size you send counts as one download.

What are the developer API and the website widget?

Included in the Business and Agency plans. In Account & security → Developer API & website widget you can make a server key for your developer or shop system (resize photos to every platform size, remove backgrounds, write captions, make AI product photos), or a website widget code to paste into your own website so visitors can resize photos in your name. Every file made counts toward your downloads. Full guide: Developers.

What are AI models and presenter videos?

In ✨ AI Studio → 🧑 AI model, pick one of 15 made-up AI models (young adults, lifestyle presenters, mid-aged professionals and senior executives), add a photo of your product, and choose what they do (hold it, use it, point at it, at a desk or counter) and the look (studio ad, lifestyle or phone-selfie style). You get a photo of the model with your real product — 1 AI credit per photo. Then press 🎙 Presenter video: write (or let AI write) what the presenter says about your product’s features, pick 8 or 15 seconds and a language, and the model talks to the camera with a natural voice (Pro, Business and Agency plans: 15 credits for 8 s, 28 for 15 s). The models are AI characters, not real people, so they describe your product like an advert presenter — they can’t pretend to be a customer or give a review (fake testimonials are against consumer law in many countries). Videos get a small “AI-generated” label by default, which Instagram, Facebook and TikTok ask for.

How do I make a slideshow video with music?

Open 🎬 Slideshow video from the menu. Add 3 photos (up to 5 — tap a slot on your phone to take a picture), pick one of the 12 templates, type a title, a line for each photo and a closing line, then choose a song. Press ▶ Play to preview, then Make my video — you get a 10–15 second MP4 for Reels, Stories, TikTok or WhatsApp. All built-in songs are PostGenX originals, free to use with no copyright claims; you can also upload your own music if you have the rights to it. Previews are free; downloading videos is included from the Starter plan and counts as one download.

How do I put text on my photo or video, like Instagram?

In the editor, use ✍ Text on photo / video: type your text, choose a look (Classic, Modern, Neon, Typewriter, Strong, Editor, Handwritten) or an effect (shadow, outline, highlight, soft box, neon glow, 3D), size, alignment and colours, then drag it anywhere. It works on videos too. To style the headline, subheadline and price, use Style → Text effect.

How does the Bulk studio work?

Open 📦 Bulk studio from the menu (Starter plan and up). Drop up to 50 product photos — or paste product links, one per line, and we copy each product's name, price and photo. Pick your brand kit, background (soft blur, brand colour, clean white or cut-out) and the sizes you need, check the preview, then click Make all posts. You get one ZIP with a folder per product. Tick Add AI captions to include ready-to-post captions too. Each size of each product counts as one download.

How do AI captions work?

In the editor, click 💬 Caption, pick the platform, language and tone, then ✨ Write with AI — PostGenX writes a caption with hashtags for that platform. Click 📋 Copy caption and paste it when you post your downloaded design from your phone. Bulk studio can add captions for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn and WhatsApp for every product. Captions don't use AI credits (Free: 5 a day, paid plans: 100 a day). Always read them before posting.

What are design variations?

On any design card click ✨ Variations and tick the versions you want — flash sale, new arrival, best seller, customer review, hiring notice, festival greeting or weekend offer. Each is saved as a new design with the same photo, logo and price, ready to tweak.

Retail or Business account — what's the difference?

Retail / Shop owner keeps things simple: snap a product photo, edit it and post. Business / Agency is for wholesalers, multi-store brands and marketing agencies: one brand kit per brand or client (logo, colours, fonts, contact and social details), a brand switcher on your designs, free client logins so each client can review and download only their own designs, and a downloadable activity log for security reviews. Switch any time in Account & security → Account type — nothing is deleted. The Agency plan (US$119 per user per month) includes up to 50 brands and 800 AI credits a month.

How do client logins work?

In a Business account, go to Team → Invite someone, choose Client and the client's brand. They get an email to create a login. Clients don't use a paid seat. They only see designs of their brand that you've shared (anything but 🔒 Private), and they can download them — nothing else in your workspace.

How do AI product videos work?

On Pro and Business, open ✨ AI Studio → 🎬 Video ad. Start from one of your AI photos (best) or upload a photo, pick a motion (light sweep, slow push-in, orbit, floating, steam, splash, festive glow…), a shape and 4, 6 or 8 seconds. In 1–4 minutes you get a silent MP4 — the camera and light move, your product stays exactly the same, and there is never any writing on it. Add your headline, price, logo and music in the editor. A video uses 10 AI credits; if it fails or doesn’t pass our quality check, the credits are returned.

What does "per user" mean?

Each person on your team uses one seat and gets their own download allowance. Add seats under Plan & billing.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Plan & billing. Switching plans applies straight away (the price difference is prorated). Cancelling keeps your plan until the end of the period you've paid for.

Where are my invoices?

Plan & billing → Invoices & card opens your secure billing portal, where you can download invoices and update your card.

👥 Teams & sharing

How do I invite staff?

Dashboard → Team → enter their email and pick a role: Admin (manages the team), Designer (creates and downloads) or Viewer (views shared designs only).

Who can see my designs?

New designs are Private. Change a design to Team can view or Team can edit from its card in the dashboard.

🔐 Account, security & privacy

How do I turn on 2-step login?

Account & security → 2-step verification → scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password and enter the 6-digit code.

How do I download or delete my data?

Account & security → Download my data gives you a ZIP of everything we hold, including your uploads. Delete my account removes it permanently (if you own the business account, the whole workspace is deleted and billing cancelled).

I forgot my password / I'm locked out

Use Forgot password? on the login page. After 5 wrong attempts an account locks for 15 minutes to keep it safe — a password reset unlocks it straight away.

🛠 Troubleshooting

The editor is slow or a button doesn't respond

Refresh the page (your work saves automatically). Use the latest Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Very large photos (over 20 MP) can be slow on older phones.

My video export failed

Keep the video under 3 minutes and 100 MB, keep the tab open while it records (it plays through once), and try Chrome or Safari.
