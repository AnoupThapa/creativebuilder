🚀 Getting started

How do I make my first post?
  1. Sign up and confirm your email (check spam if it doesn't arrive).
  2. Click New design, upload a photo, then pick a template or type your offer.
  3. Choose a size (e.g. Instagram Post) at the top of the canvas and click Download.
Which sizes are supported?

Instagram post, portrait & story/reels, Facebook post, story & cover, LinkedIn post & banner, Pinterest, X (Twitter), TikTok, YouTube thumbnail & Shorts, and WhatsApp status. Use Generate all platforms to get every size in one ZIP.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — PostForge works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. For the most comfortable editing use a tablet or computer, but everything, including downloads, works on a phone.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or promotions folder. In the dashboard, click Resend email on the yellow banner. Still nothing after 10 minutes? Contact us below.

🎨 Designing posts

How do I move text, the badge or the logo?

Click it on the design and drag it. Drag the round corner handle to resize. For precise moves use the arrow pad and sliders in the Layout tab, or pick a one-click Layout (Classic, Centred, Text on top, Middle) under Style.

How do I remove the background from a product photo?

Switch on Remove background under your photo. If anything needs fixing, click ✨ Refine: drag a Subject box around the product for busy backgrounds, then use the Keep and Erase brushes or Magic erase. Choose a backdrop (gradient, spotlight, blurred photo) behind the cut-out.

Can I use my own logo, colours and fonts?

Yes. Save a Brand kit (logo, colour, phone, website) in the dashboard or the editor's Brand Kit tab and apply it in one click. Pick heading and text fonts under Style.

Can I make video posts?

On the Pro plan, upload a video (MP4, MOV or WEBM), add your branding, choose MP4 video in Export and download. Videos come out as MP4 (H.264 + AAC) — the format Instagram, Facebook and TikTok expect.

How do I make a customer-review post?

Choose Customer review at the top of the left panel, type the review, the customer's name and star rating, and add their photo if you have permission to use it.

⬇ Downloads & allowances

What counts as one image?

Every file you download counts once. A ZIP with 4 sizes uses 4. Editing and previewing are always unlimited.

When does my allowance reset?

Daily limits reset at midnight in the time zone set in Account & security. Monthly limits reset on the 1st.

Why is there a watermark?

Free-trial downloads carry a small watermark. Any paid plan removes it.

My download looks blurry

Use a photo at least 1080 px on its shortest side, and choose HD 2× (Starter/Pro) or Print 3× (Pro) in Export.

📣 Posting to Facebook & Instagram

How do I connect my Facebook Page and Instagram?

Go to Social posts and click Connect Facebook & Instagram (owners and admins only). Facebook opens its own login window — you sign in there, choose the Pages and Instagram accounts to share, and approve. PostForge never sees your password.

Why doesn't my Instagram account show up?

Instagram posting works for Business or Creator accounts that are linked to a Facebook Page. In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → switch to Professional, then link it to your Facebook Page. Connect again in PostForge and tick the Page.

How do I post or schedule?

Open a design, click 📣 Post, tick the accounts, write a caption, then choose Post now or Schedule and pick a date and time. Scheduled posts are listed under Social posts, where you can change the time or cancel them.

Which sizes work on Instagram?

Feed posts must be between portrait 4:5 and landscape 1.91:1 — use Instagram Post (square) or Instagram Portrait. Tall 9:16 designs (Story/Reels size) can be posted as an Instagram Story, or as a Reel when the design is a video.

Does posting use my download allowance?

Yes — each post counts as one download, however many accounts it goes to.

A post failed — what now?

Open Social posts → Posted & history; the reason is shown under the post. If it says the connection expired, click Connect Facebook & Instagram again, then Retry.

💳 Plans & billing

What do the plans include?

Free — try the editor plus 3 AI product photos. Starter — US$12 per user per month: 5 images a day (up to 100 a month), HD quality, all templates, your own videos up to 30 seconds, 40 AI photos a month. Pro — US$29 per user per month: 15 images a day (up to 300 a month), video posts, print quality, 5 brand kits, 150 AI photos a month. Business — US$79 per user per month: 50 a day (up to 1,000 a month), 20 brand kits, 500 AI photos a month. Yearly billing gives you 2 months free.

How do AI product photos work?

Open ✨ AI Studio, pick your industry and a style, then add a photo of your product (or paste its web link). The AI makes a professional picture around your real product — without any writing on it. Click Add text & logo to finish it in the editor. One picture uses one AI credit. If the AI fails, the credit is returned automatically. Need more? Buy a pack of 100 credits for US$10 (never expires).

How do AI product videos work?

On Pro and Business, open ✨ AI Studio → 🎬 Video ad. Start from one of your AI photos (best) or upload a photo, pick a motion (light sweep, slow push-in, orbit, floating, steam, splash, festive glow…), a shape and 4, 6 or 8 seconds. In 1–4 minutes you get a silent MP4 — the camera and light move, your product stays exactly the same, and there is never any writing on it. Add your headline, price, logo and music in the editor. A video uses 10 AI credits; if it fails or doesn’t pass our quality check, the credits are returned.

What does "per user" mean?

Each person on your team uses one seat and gets their own download allowance. Add seats under Plan & billing.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Open Plan & billing. Switching plans applies straight away (the price difference is prorated). Cancelling keeps your plan until the end of the period you've paid for.

Where are my invoices?

Plan & billing → Invoices & card opens your secure billing portal, where you can download invoices and update your card.

👥 Teams & sharing

How do I invite staff?

Dashboard → Team → enter their email and pick a role: Admin (manages the team), Designer (creates and downloads) or Viewer (views shared designs only).

Who can see my designs?

New designs are Private. Change a design to Team can view or Team can edit from its card in the dashboard.

🔐 Account, security & privacy

How do I turn on 2-step login?

Account & security → 2-step verification → scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Authy or 1Password and enter the 6-digit code.

How do I download or delete my data?

Account & security → Download my data gives you a ZIP of everything we hold, including your uploads. Delete my account removes it permanently (if you own the business account, the whole workspace is deleted and billing cancelled).

I forgot my password / I'm locked out

Use Forgot password? on the login page. After 5 wrong attempts an account locks for 15 minutes to keep it safe — a password reset unlocks it straight away.

🛠 Troubleshooting

The editor is slow or a button doesn't respond

Refresh the page (your work saves automatically). Use the latest Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. Very large photos (over 20 MP) can be slow on older phones.

My video export failed

Keep the video under 3 minutes and 100 MB, keep the tab open while it records (it plays through once), and try Chrome or Safari.
