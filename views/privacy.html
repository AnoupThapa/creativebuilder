Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Who we are

PostGenX is an online tool that helps small businesses create social media posts and publish them to their own social media accounts. Contact us any time through the contact form.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, business name, time zone and an encrypted (hashed) password.
  • Your content: photos, videos, logos and designs you upload or create, captions you write, and posts you schedule.
  • Connected social accounts: if you connect Facebook or Instagram, we store the names and IDs of the Facebook Pages and Instagram Business/Creator accounts you choose to share, and an access token that lets PostGenX publish to them. The token is stored encrypted. We never see or store your Facebook or Instagram password — you sign in on Facebook’s own page.
  • Usage and security records: downloads and posts (to apply your plan’s allowance), sign-ins, and basic device information for security.
  • Website statistics: page visits counted without cookies; IP addresses are not stored.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: saving your designs, creating images and videos, and publishing posts only when you or your team ask us to (now or at the time you schedule).
  • To keep accounts secure, send account emails (e.g. password resets, invites), and answer support requests.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or read your social media messages, followers or insights.

Facebook and Instagram data

PostGenX uses Meta’s official APIs only to list the Pages and Instagram accounts you authorised and to publish the posts you create. You can disconnect an account at any time in Social posts → Disconnect, and you can also remove PostGenX in your Facebook settings under Business integrations. When you disconnect, we delete the stored token for that account.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run PostGenX: our hosting provider, our email provider, our payment provider (for paid plans) and, when you publish, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) receives the post you send to it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can download a copy of your data or delete your account yourself at any time from Account & security. Deleting removes your account, designs, uploads, connected social accounts and scheduled posts. Backups are overwritten within 14 days. Posts already published on Facebook or Instagram stay there until you delete them on those platforms.

Data deletion request

To ask us to delete your data instead, use the contact form with the subject “Delete my data” from the email address on your account. We complete requests within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you by email or in the app.
